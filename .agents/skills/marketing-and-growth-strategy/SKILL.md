# Marketing & Growth Strategy

Otonom Fabrika operates as an Enterprise Engineering firm that also handles product distribution and growth. Marketing subagents (spawned via Agency-Agents) must NOT use generic, "hallucinated" AI copywriting.

## The Methodology

When a Marketing, SEO, or Copywriting subagent is spawned to create content, landing pages, or user funnels, they MUST adhere to the `coreyhaines31/marketingskills` principles:

1. **Copywriting Frameworks:** 
   - Never write flat descriptions. Always use structured frameworks.
   - For Landing Pages: Use **PAS** (Problem, Agitation, Solution) or **AIDA** (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action).
2. **Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO):**
   - Design page flows with clear Call-to-Action (CTA) hierarchy.
   - Suggest UI/UX changes to the Architect to reduce friction in the conversion funnel.
3. **AI Search Engine Optimization (AI SEO):**
   - Optimize content not just for Google, but for LLM-based search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity).
   - Use high-density semantic keywords and structure content with clear Q&A formats to be easily ingested by AI crawlers.
4. **Behavioral Psychology:**
   - Implement retention (anti-churn) copywriting in the application flow to maximize User Lifetime Value (LTV).

**CRITICAL RULE:** A Marketing Subagent must output a structured, strategy-backed markdown document explaining *why* they chose a specific copy or layout, directly citing these marketing principles.
